Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

Emberluth is a free app for string players: a tuner, practice tools, instrument care and a practice log, set in a painted common room. It has no advertisements, analytics or tracking, and no user accounts.

1. Microphone and audio

Emberluth uses the microphone only while the tuner is open, to detect the pitch of the string you play. Sound is analysed on your phone in real time. The app does not record, save, upload or send microphone audio. You can use every other part of the app without allowing the microphone.

2. What is stored on your phone

Emberluth keeps the following on your phone, in the app's own storage:

  • Tuner settings: instrument, tuning, custom tunings, reference pitch, modes and display preferences.
  • Practice data: metronome tempo and the start time of a running practice timer.
  • Things you write: practice log entries, goals and notes, string-change records and instrument notes.
  • Room state: which instruments you tuned on which day, whether you have seen the opening, whether the room's fire and rain sounds are on, and the date of your last backup file.

None of this is sent to the developer or anyone else by the app.

3. Collection and sharing

Emberluth does not collect personal information, does not use analytics or advertising services, and does not share data with third parties.

4. Permissions

  • Microphone: for real-time pitch detection in the tuner.
  • Modify audio settings: lets the app's built-in web view open the microphone and play its own sounds (reference tones, the metronome, fire and rain). The app does not change your volume.
  • Vibration: for optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • Internet: required by Android for the app's built-in web view. The app works fully offline and does not use the network to send your data.

5. Feedback emails

The Send feedback option opens your own email app with a draft that includes the app version, your phone model, Android version and the selected instrument and tuning. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it in the app or uninstall Emberluth. If Google backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android includes Emberluth's data in your own Google account backup and restores it when you reinstall the app or move to a new phone; this is handled by Android and Google, and the developer has no access to it. You can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.

You can also save a backup file from Settings › Back up and restore and keep it wherever you choose, such as your own cloud storage or email. Restoring from a file replaces the data on the phone.

7. Children

Emberluth does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.
